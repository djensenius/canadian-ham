# App icon concept round 3: top-down hex beam studies

Four exploratory raster concepts are generated by `render_concepts.py`. The generator keeps all output in `design/app-icon/concepts/` and does not modify the approved source/export asset pipeline.

Round 3 keeps the round-2 brush technique: each mark is a solid filled sumi-e ink body with a pressed landing, smoother low-frequency edge irregularity, and true even-odd hihaku cutouts for flying-white dry-brush tails. This round replaces the round-2 concept folders with top-down hex-beam studies plus one side-view alternative, because the owner wanted the hex beam seen from above and the round-2 `05-hex-beam` read too much like a leaf.

## Concepts

1. **Hex top:** top-down hex beam with six straight spreader strokes, a small pressed hub dot, a closed hexagon perimeter in three confident strokes, and two pale zigzag W/M wire elements on opposite halves.
2. **Hex top ensō:** the same top-down hex beam reduced slightly inside a thinner open ensō circle with one dry-brush gap.
3. **Hex top minimal:** small-size test mark with only the bold closed hexagon perimeter, six straight spreaders, and hub dot.
4. **Hex side:** side/three-quarter alternative with a mast, hub, six thin umbrella-like spreaders, and a flattened hexagon wire outline across the tips.

## Round-3 render-then-critique passes

1. **Pass 1:** Regenerated the four-concept sheet and opened both `comparison.png` and `closeups.png`. Brush read: yes, the marks still had solid ink bodies, pressed starts, and dry-brush tails rather than hairlines. Hex-beam read: the top-down concepts clearly moved away from the round-2 leaf/cannabis silhouette, but the side-view alternative was undersized at 60 px. Exclusion check: no concentric signal arcs; the top-down marks read more as hex frames than wheels because the perimeter was a hexagon, not a circle.
2. **Pass 2:** Enlarged the side/three-quarter hex beam and recentered its umbrella spreaders, then opened both sheets again. Brush read: yes; the spreaders stayed thin and straight instead of leaf-blade shaped. Hex-beam read: side view became legible at 60 px, with the flattened hexagon wire now spanning about two-thirds of the tile. Exclusion check: no web of extra wires, no snowflake branching, and no leaf-shaped spokes.
3. **Pass 3:** Reduced the perimeter stroke landing pressure and nudged dry-brush lift-off later to smooth the top-down hex edges, then regenerated and opened both sheets. Brush read: yes, the closeups retain hihaku tails while the bodies are less blobby and less faceted. Hex-beam read: `01-hex-top` carries the full top-down concept with W/M wires, `02-hex-top-enso` frames the same mark without becoming a signal icon, `03-hex-top-minimal` remains readable at 60 px and still reduces to a hex/spoke mark at 32 px, and `04-hex-side` reads as an upturned-umbrella hex beam rather than a leaf.
