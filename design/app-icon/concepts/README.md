# App icon concept round: sumi-e ham antennas

Four exploratory raster concepts are generated by `render_concepts.py`. The generator keeps all output in `design/app-icon/concepts/` and does not modify the approved source/export asset pipeline.

## Concepts

1. **Yagi beam:** diagonal mast and directional beam, no broadcast arcs.
2. **Lattice tower:** triangular ham tower with a compact beam on top.
3. **Ground-plane vertical:** center whip with sloping radials and a faint ink-wash secondary stroke.
4. **Wire dipole:** sagging wire between two supports over a single dry horizon stroke.

## Render-then-critique passes

1. **Pass 1:** The four subjects are ham-specific and avoid podcast/Wi-Fi arcs, and the bristle-track construction is visible at large size. Critique: the ink is too grey and spidery overall, the ground-plane and dipole do not fill enough of the tile, the ground-plane wash ends in a distracting dot, and the 60 px versions read more like thin technical sketches than confident brush marks.
2. **Pass 2:** Increased bristle density/opacity and removed pooling from the grey wash, which gives the Yagi and tower enough weight at 60 px without looking like clean vector art. Critique: the vertical ground-plane is still too narrow and faint at small sizes, and the wire dipole reads slightly box-like because the support strokes dominate the sagging wire.
3. **Pass 3:** Enlarged and darkened the ground-plane whip/radials, leaned the dipole supports outward, deepened the dipole sag, and shortened the comparison labels. Critique: the concepts now read as distinct ham antenna forms at 60 px, remain non-podcast because there are no concentric broadcast arcs, and show bristle streaks/dry breaks at large size; 32 px naturally simplifies but each silhouette still has a recognizable antenna structure.
