# content/explanations

Purpose: original explanation records keyed to official question IDs.

Belongs here:

- Original explanations for why answers are correct or incorrect.
- Citations to question IDs, RBR-4, and other verified sources.

Must not go here:

- Reworded or copied official ISED question text.
- Copied third-party study-guide prose, diagrams, examples, or tables.
- Generated question-bank JSON.

Relevant docs: [`../../docs/requirements.md`](../../docs/requirements.md), [`../../docs/architecture.md`](../../docs/architecture.md), [`../../docs/content-licensing.md`](../../docs/content-licensing.md).
