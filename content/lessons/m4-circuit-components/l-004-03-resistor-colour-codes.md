## Learning objectives

- Decode four-band resistor values.
- Identify tolerance-band meaning.
- Check decoded values against expected circuit use.

## Key concepts

- First bands give significant digits and multiplier.
- The tolerance band describes allowed variation from the nominal value.
- Colour-code reading is a lookup skill plus place-value arithmetic.

## Coverage

- B-004-006 — Resistor colour codes and tolerance (questions B-004-006-001 through B-004-006-011)

## Teaching notes

- Reference sheet: use the resistor colour-code table if present in the exam reference.
- Provide original practice values and have learners read left to right.
- Warn that lighting and body colour can make real resistors ambiguous.
