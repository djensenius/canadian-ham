## Learning objectives

- Predict current and resistance behaviour in parallel branches.
- Calculate DC input power from voltage and current.
- Select component ratings with margin for heat and load.

## Key concepts

- Parallel branch currents add at the source.
- Total parallel resistance is less than the smallest branch resistance.
- Power ratings describe heat-handling capacity, not resistance value.

## Coverage

- B-005-005 — Parallel circuit relationships (questions B-005-005-001 through B-005-005-011)
- B-005-006 — Power ratings and DC input power (questions B-005-006-001 through B-005-006-011)

## Teaching notes

- Reference sheet: P = E × I and parallel-resistance relationships apply.

- Relate rating choices to station power supplies and dummy loads.
