## Learning objectives

- Set keyers and microphones for readable signals.
- Explain the difference between analog and digital information.
- Describe digipeaters and digital radio systems at a block level.

## Key concepts

- Operator controls affect transmitted quality.
- Digital systems encode information as discrete states.
- Relays can forward packet or digital traffic under amateur rules.

## Coverage

- B-003-014 — Keyer and microphone adjustments (questions B-003-014-001 through B-003-014-011)
- B-003-015 — Digital voice, data, and packet relays (questions B-003-015-001 through B-003-015-011)
