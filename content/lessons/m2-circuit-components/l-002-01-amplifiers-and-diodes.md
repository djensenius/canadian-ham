## Learning objectives

- State what an amplifier does to a signal.
- Explain why linearity matters in radio circuits.
- Identify common diode jobs such as rectification, detection, and protection.

## Key concepts

- Amplifiers increase signal level while preserving useful information.
- Non-linearity creates distortion and unwanted products.
- Diodes conduct mainly one way and can protect against polarity mistakes.
