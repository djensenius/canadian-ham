## Learning objectives

- Compare bipolar transistor, FET, and triode control terminals.
- Recognize why different active devices appear in RF circuits.
- Describe control of a larger current by a smaller signal.

## Key concepts

- Bipolar transistors use base, collector, and emitter.
- FETs use gate, drain, and source.
- Vacuum tubes still appear where high RF power or voltage handling matters.

- B-004-003 — Bipolar transistor basics (questions B-004-003-001 through B-004-003-011)
- B-004-004 — Field-effect transistor basics (questions B-004-004-001 through B-004-004-011)
