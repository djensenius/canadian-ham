## Learning objectives

- Identify causes of feedline loss.
- Interpret SWR readings without treating them as a complete efficiency measure.
- Explain what a tuner or matching network changes.

## Key concepts

- Loss rises with frequency, length, and unsuitable cable choices.
- Low SWR indicates a match at the measurement point.
- Tuners transform impedance so equipment can operate properly.

## Coverage

- B-006-004 — Feedline losses and length choices (questions B-006-004-001 through B-006-004-010)
- B-006-005 — SWR readings and measurement faults (questions B-006-005-001 through B-006-005-011)
- B-006-006 — Matching networks and antenna tuners (questions B-006-006-001 through B-006-006-011)

## Teaching notes

- Reference sheet: SWR and impedance-matching relationships apply.
- Show an original block sketch: transmitter, meter, tuner, feedline, antenna.
- Stress that matching at the transmitter does not remove all feedline loss.
