## Learning objectives

- Describe how parasitic elements shape antenna directivity.
- Estimate quarter-wave and half-wave element lengths.
- Recognize Yagi driven elements, directors, reflectors, and traps.

## Key concepts

- Parasitic elements are energized by nearby RF fields.
- Directors and reflectors redistribute radiation strength.
- Trap designs allow operation on multiple bands.

## Coverage

- B-006-009 — Parasitic elements and directivity (questions B-006-009-001 through B-006-009-011)
- B-006-010 — Quarter- and half-wave antenna lengths (questions B-006-010-001 through B-006-010-011)
- B-006-011 — Yagi dimensions and multiband traps (questions B-006-011-001 through B-006-011-011)
