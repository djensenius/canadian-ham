## Learning objectives

- Relate element orientation to wave polarization.
- Adjust dipole length to move resonant frequency.
- Estimate wavelength from frequency.

## Key concepts

- Horizontal and vertical polarization follow the electric-field orientation.
- Longer antennas resonate lower; shorter antennas resonate higher.
- Wavelength estimates guide first-cut antenna dimensions.
