## Learning objectives

- Compare dipole, random-wire, quad, and loop antenna patterns.
- Estimate quad driven-element length for a target band.
- Identify tradeoffs between simple wires and directional loops.

## Key concepts

- Radiation patterns describe relative strength by direction.
- Loop and quad antennas use closed conductors with practical support needs.
- Random-wire antennas usually require matching and careful RF grounding.

- B-006-012 — Dipole and random-wire patterns (questions B-006-012-001 through B-006-012-009)
- B-006-013 — Quad and loop antennas (questions B-006-013-001 through B-006-013-011)
