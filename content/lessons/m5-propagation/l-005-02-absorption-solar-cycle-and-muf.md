## Learning objectives

- Describe daytime absorption and multipath fading.
- Relate sunspots and solar flux to ionization.
- Use MUF, critical frequency, and LUF as planning concepts.

## Key concepts

- The D region can absorb lower HF energy during daylight.
- Solar activity changes the range of usable HF frequencies.
- Usable-frequency terms describe limits, not guarantees.
