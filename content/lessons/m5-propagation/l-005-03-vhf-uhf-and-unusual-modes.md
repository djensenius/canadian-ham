## Learning objectives

- Explain sporadic E and tropospheric effects on VHF/UHF.
- Recognize scatter and ducting clues.
- Choose operating expectations for weak or distorted signals.

## Key concepts

- Six metres can behave like HF during some ionospheric events.
- Tropospheric bending and ducting depend on weather-related gradients.
- Scatter modes often sound weak or distorted.

## Coverage

- B-007-007 — Six-metre and tropospheric propagation (questions B-007-007-001 through B-007-007-011)
- B-007-008 — Scatter and unusual propagation modes (questions B-007-008-001 through B-007-008-011)

## Teaching notes

- Reference sheet: propagation mode diagrams may apply.
- Use local, original examples such as nearby repeaters versus distant openings.
- Reinforce that unusual openings are opportunities, not everyday coverage.
