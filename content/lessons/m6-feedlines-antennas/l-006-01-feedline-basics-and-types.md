## Learning objectives

- Explain the job of a feedline between radio and antenna.
- Compare coaxial, twin-lead, and open-wire construction.
- Choose feedline based on surroundings and common-mode concerns.

## Key concepts

- Characteristic impedance belongs to line geometry, not line length alone.
- Shielding and balance affect noise pickup and installation tolerance.
- Ferrites and routing can reduce unwanted common-mode current.

- B-006-001 — Transmission-line role and impedance (questions B-006-001-001 through B-006-001-011)
