## Learning objectives

- Identify harmonic-emission problems and likely symptoms.
- Select low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and notch filter roles.
- Place filters where they address the actual unwanted energy.

## Key concepts

- Harmonics are integer multiples of a transmitted frequency.
- Filters attenuate selected ranges and pass others.
- Correct filter type depends on what must be blocked or passed.
