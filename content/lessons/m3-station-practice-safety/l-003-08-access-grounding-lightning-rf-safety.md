## Learning objectives

- Prevent unauthorized station use at home and mobile.
- Apply grounding, bonding, fusing, and shock-protection practices.
- Protect equipment from lightning and cable surges.
- Recognize extra precautions for UHF and microwave work.

## Key concepts

- Physical and electrical controls both protect people.
- Bonding reduces dangerous voltage differences.
- Lightning protection is a system, not a single part.
- Higher-frequency RF can create exposure hazards near antennas and amplifiers.
