## Learning objectives

- Identify boom, driven element, reflector, and director in a simple Yagi.
- Rank emission bandwidths from narrow to wide.
- Interpret common receiver specifications and waterfall displays.

## Key concepts

- Mechanical and radiating parts have different jobs.
- Bandwidth affects spectrum occupancy and interference risk.
- Sensitivity, selectivity, and visual displays describe receiver performance.

- B-003-009 — Yagi antenna parts (questions B-003-009-001 through B-003-009-004)
- B-003-010 — Receiver and emission specifications (questions B-003-010-001 through B-003-010-011)
