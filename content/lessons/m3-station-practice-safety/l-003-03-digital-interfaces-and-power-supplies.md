## Learning objectives

- Explain the role of a sound-card interface in computer-based modes.
- Identify transformer, rectifier, filter, and regulator blocks in a linear supply.
- Relate overvoltage monitoring to equipment protection.

## Key concepts

- Audio interfaces pass tones and may provide isolation or level matching.
- Linear supplies convert AC to filtered DC in stages.
- Protection circuits monitor outputs that feed radio equipment.
