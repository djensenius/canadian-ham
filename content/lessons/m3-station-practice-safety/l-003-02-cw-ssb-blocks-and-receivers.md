## Learning objectives

- Describe the oscillator, buffer, and amplifier roles in a CW transmitter.
- Trace SSB/CW receiver stages from antenna to speaker.
- Explain how SSB transmitters create and filter sidebands.

## Key concepts

- Oscillator stability affects transmitted signal quality.
- Mixers translate signals while preserving information.
- Balanced modulators and filters shape SSB output.
