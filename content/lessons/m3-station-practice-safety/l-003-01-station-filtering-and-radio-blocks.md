## Learning objectives

- Place low-pass filters in a basic HF station.
- Trace audio through an FM transmitter.
- Name the main blocks of a superheterodyne receiver.

## Key concepts

- Low-pass filtering belongs near the source of unwanted harmonics.
- FM transmitters turn microphone audio into frequency deviation.
- Receiver front ends, mixers, IF stages, and detectors divide the work.
