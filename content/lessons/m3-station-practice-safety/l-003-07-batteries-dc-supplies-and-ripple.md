## Learning objectives

- Identify common station battery characteristics.
- Select supply current capacity for transceivers.
- Explain how poor filtering can affect transmitted signals.

## Key concepts

- Vehicle and station batteries can supply high current.
- A supply must handle expected current at the required voltage.
- Ripple from inadequate filtering can modulate a signal.

- B-003-017 — Power supply selection and ripple (questions B-003-017-001 through B-003-017-011)
