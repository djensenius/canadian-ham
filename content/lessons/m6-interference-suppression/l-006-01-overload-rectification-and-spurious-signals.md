## Learning objectives

- Distinguish receiver overload from transmitter faults.
- Explain audio rectification in nearby equipment.
- Recognize intermodulation and spurious emission symptoms.

## Key concepts

- Strong desired signals can overload a receiver front end.
- Non-linear devices can turn RF into audible interference.
- Unwanted products can arise in transmitters or affected receivers.
