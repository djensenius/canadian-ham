## Learning objectives

- Explain contact confirmation and basic station records.
- Use azimuth and grid-locator concepts for contacts.
- Relate location data to antennas and propagation.

## Key concepts

- QSL records confirm contact details.
- Azimuthal maps help visualize direction from the station.
- Grid squares identify approximate location for contests and awards.
