## Learning objectives

- Recognize basic Morse calling flow.
- Interpret RST readability, strength, and tone reports.
- Use common Q signals without turning them into voice jargon.

## Key concepts

- Morse procedure emphasizes repeatable, compact exchanges.
- RST reports separate readability from signal strength and tone.
- Q signals are standardized shortcuts, especially useful on CW.

## Coverage

- B-002-005 — Morse calling procedures (questions B-002-005-001 through B-002-005-011)
- B-002-006 — RST signal reports (questions B-002-006-001 through B-002-006-011)
- B-002-007 — Q signals and operating abbreviations (questions B-002-007-001 through B-002-007-011)

- Reference sheet: Q signal and RST lists may apply if provided.
- Use original practice exchanges and avoid claiming Morse is required for Basic.
- Connect signal reports to receiver and propagation conditions.
