## Learning objectives

- Explain who may install or modify equipment for others.
- Identify allowed station uses and construction privileges.
- Assign responsibility for station control and supervision.

## Key concepts

- Privileges depend on certificate qualifications and station control.
- Amateur stations have limits on purpose and equipment use.
- The owner and control operator both need clear responsibility boundaries.
