## Learning objectives

- Avoid harmful interference and respect primary/secondary service status.
- Use emergency communication exceptions correctly.
- Apply message privacy, compensation, identification, and third-party limits.

## Key concepts

- Amateur communication is not broadcasting to the public.
- Emergency authority is narrow and purpose-driven.
- Call signs and identification make station accountability visible.
