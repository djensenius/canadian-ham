## Learning objectives

- Identify antenna-siting consultation responsibilities.
- Explain the purpose of RF exposure limits.
- Use EMCAB-2 criteria when assessing interference complaints.

## Key concepts

- Antenna structures can involve land-use authority and public-consultation processes.
- Safety Code 6 limits protect people from excessive RF exposure.
- Interference responsibility depends on field strength, immunity, and equipment category.
