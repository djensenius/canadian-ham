## Learning objectives

- Recognize AC frequency and the audio-frequency range.
- Convert common dB changes into power-ratio intuition.
- Compare inductor and capacitor behaviour in AC circuits.
- Combine simple inductance and capacitance values.

## Key concepts

- Frequency is cycles per second.
- A 3 dB change is approximately a factor of two in power.
- Inductors and capacitors oppose AC differently as frequency changes.
- Series and parallel combinations depend on component type.
