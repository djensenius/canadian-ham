## Learning objectives

- Use transformer ratios for voltage, current, and impedance reasoning.
- Describe resonance, bandwidth, and Q in tuned circuits.
- Connect voltmeters, ammeters, ohmmeters, and multimeters safely.

## Key concepts

- Transformer winding ratios set ideal voltage and current relationships.
- Resonance occurs when inductive and capacitive effects balance.
- Meters must be placed according to what they measure.
