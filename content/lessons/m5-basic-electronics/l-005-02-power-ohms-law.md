## Learning objectives

- Explain electrical power as a rate of energy use.
- Apply Ohm’s law to solve for voltage, current, or resistance.
- Choose the correct formula from the known quantities.

## Key concepts

- Power is measured in watts.
- Ohm’s law connects voltage, current, and resistance.
- Algebraic rearrangement is safer than memorizing answer patterns.
