## Learning objectives

- Convert between common radio units and prefixes.
- Distinguish conductive and insulating materials.
- Relate electric charge flow to current in a circuit.

## Key concepts

- Hz, kHz, MHz, mA, A, and other prefix shifts.
- Conductors provide easy charge paths; insulators resist charge flow.
- Current is the movement of charge through a complete path.

## Coverage

- B-005-001 — Units, prefixes, and frequency conversion (questions B-005-001-001 through B-005-001-011)
- B-005-002 — Conductors, insulators, and current (questions B-005-002-001 through B-005-002-011)

## Teaching notes

- Reference sheet: use metric-prefix relationships and frequency unit conversions.
- Use original number-line examples; do not quote bank wording.
- Emphasize checking units before doing arithmetic.
