## Learning objectives

- Make concise voice contacts on repeaters and simplex channels.
- Use phonetics for clear identification.
- Listen first and use only the power needed.

## Key concepts

- Repeaters extend range but require shared-channel courtesy.
- Phonetics reduce call-sign errors.
- Good operating starts before pressing transmit.
