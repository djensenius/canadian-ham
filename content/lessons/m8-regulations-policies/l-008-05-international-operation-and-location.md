## Learning objectives

- Apply international message rules for amateur contacts.
- Relate operating location to ITU regions and applicable limits.
- Recognize when Canadian operators are subject to foreign or regional rules.

## Key concepts

- International amateur communication depends on reciprocal permissions and message type.
- Station location can change which band limits apply.
- Maritime or foreign operation should be verified before transmitting.

## Coverage

- B-001-020 — International communications rules (questions B-001-020-001 through B-001-020-005)
- B-001-021 — ITU regions and station location (questions B-001-021-001 through B-001-021-009)
