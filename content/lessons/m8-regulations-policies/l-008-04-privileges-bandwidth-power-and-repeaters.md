## Learning objectives

- Match operator qualifications to frequency and station privileges.
- Respect bandwidth and power limits by band and mode.
- Recognize repeater, automatic, stability, and modulation-quality requirements.

## Key concepts

- Privileges are tied to qualifications and control-operator status.
- Bandwidth and power limits manage shared-spectrum use.
- Automatic retransmission and signal-quality controls have specific rule constraints.
