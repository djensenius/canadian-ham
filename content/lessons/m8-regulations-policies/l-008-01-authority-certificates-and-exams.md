## Learning objectives

- Identify the legal sources and ISED role behind amateur radio rules.
- Maintain certificate and address records correctly.
- Recognize eligibility, examination, fee, and false-statement rules.

## Key concepts

- The certificate grants operating privileges under Canadian rules.
- Records and truthful applications are regulatory obligations.
- Prohibited transmissions can carry serious penalties.

## Coverage

- B-001-001 — Legal authorities and ISED roles (questions B-001-001-001 through B-001-001-004)
- B-001-002 — Certificate records and validity (questions B-001-002-001 through B-001-002-009)
- B-001-003 — Prohibited transmissions and penalties (questions B-001-003-001 through B-001-003-008)
- B-001-004 — Basic certificate eligibility (questions B-001-004-001 through B-001-004-007)
- B-001-022 — Examiners, fees, and false statements (questions B-001-022-001 through B-001-022-005)

## Teaching notes

- Reference sheet: regulatory source names may be listed; do not copy full legal text.
- Use a timeline from exam to certificate to address updates.
- Keep fee and examiner details as citations to current official material during full authoring.
